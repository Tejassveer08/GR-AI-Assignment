## What You See on the "Segments" Page

### Overview

The "Segments" page gives you a detailed look at your customer base, split into meaningful groups or segments. Each segment groups customers with similar characteristics and behaviors, making it easier to target them with the right strategies.

### Customer Segments Table

This table summarizes key information about each segment, including:

- **Segment Name:** The description of each group (e.g. *At-Risk Bargain Hunters*, *Cost-Sensitive*).
- **Churn Rate:** The percentage of customers in the segment likely to stop buying (**higher means more are leaving**).
- **Lifetime Value:** The average value each customer brings (in dollars).
- **Total Purchases:** Average purchases made by customers in the group.
- **Discount Usage Rate:** How much this group uses discounts and coupons.
- **Returns Rate:** How often products are returned by this segment.

For example:
- *At-Risk Bargain Hunters*: Churn Rate 38.6%, Lifetime Value $776  
- *Cost-Sensitive*: Churn Rate 28.9%, Lifetime Value $1,416

### Churn Rate by Segment (Bar Chart)

Below the table, a visual bar chart lets you quickly compare the churn rate between segments. Taller bars mean higher churn risk.

### How to Use & Analyze It

- **See Who’s at Risk:** Segments with higher churn rates need your attention—consider targeted offers, support, or loyalty incentives.
- **Weigh Value vs. Risk:** If a segment both spends a lot (*high Lifetime Value*) and has a high churn rate, it deserves priority for retention.
- **Compare Segment Profiles:** Lower value, high-churn groups (like "At-Risk Bargain Hunters") may not be cost-effective to retain aggressively.
- **Visualize Opportunities:** The chart makes it easy to spot where your biggest customer losses are coming from.

### What is Predicted?

This view predicts the *future* churn risk for groups of customers, not just individuals. By analyzing past behavior and patterns, the app identifies which segments you should focus on for business growth and retention.

## Summary

With this application, you can:
- **Identify important customer segments** and understand their needs.
- **Visualize and compare churn risk** across segments.
- **Prioritize actions** to keep your most valuable and at-risk customers happy.

The insights here empower you to make smarter marketing, sales, and support decisions—all from your own customer data.
